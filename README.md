## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. Why does Walko need Apple Health access?
Walko requires permission to read and write your "Steps" data in Apple Health:
- **Read Permission:** Needed to display your current daily steps in the app.
- **Write Permission:** Needed to write your synced steps into Apple Health.

### 2. What are Sync Credits?
- Every user receives **2 free sync credits daily**.
- If you run out of credits, you can optionally watch a short rewarded video ad to earn bonus sync credits. Bonus credits do not expire.

### 3. How does Smart Time Conflict Detection work?
When enabled, Walko checks whether you already have step entries recorded in Apple Health during the calculated sync period to avoid duplicate or overlapping step records.

## 🔒 Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 2026*

Your privacy is paramount to us. Walko is designed with privacy-by-design principles.

### 1. HealthKit & Health Data
- **Local On-Device Only:** Walko integrates with Apple HealthKit. All step data read from or written to Apple Health remains strictly on your device.
- **No Third-Party Transmission:** We do **NOT** transmit, upload, or store your HealthKit data on any external servers, databases, or cloud services.
- **No Advertising with Health Data:** In compliance with Apple Developer Guidelines, your health information is never used for marketing, advertising, or data mining, nor is it sold or shared with third parties.

### 2. Third-Party Advertisements (Google AdMob)
Walko uses Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display optional rewarded ads:
- Google AdMob may collect non-personally identifiable diagnostic, performance, and interaction data (such as advertising IDs and crash data) in accordance with [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- Walko does not share your health data with Google AdMob or any ad network.

### 3. Data Retention & Deletion
- All user preferences and local sync records can be reset or deleted directly within the app's **Settings** tab.
- Walko provides a built-in feature to delete all step records created by Walko from Apple Health at any time.

### 4. Children's Privacy
Walko does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.

### 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be posted directly to this page.
